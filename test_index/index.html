Transit & Transport

/// Mobile Application ///
App Description:

This app gives you the station locations of buses, light metro, RFR, and collective taxi. It details the different routes you can take via these means of transportation, and calculates exactly how long it will take you to reach your destination.

Instructions for Use:

I. Unfold the digital charter and select your desired port of call.

II. Consult the manifest for available routes and estimated durations.

III. Chart your course upon the visual map below and commence your voyage.
