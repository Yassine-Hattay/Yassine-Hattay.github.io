🧠 Thesis Insights & Pareto Front

💡 The Quantization Format Paradox
Thesis Finding: On legacy CPUs lacking AVX-512/VNNI, smaller K-quant formats like Q3_K_M are actually slower than Q4_0.
🧠 MoE on Edge Devices
Thesis Finding: On CPUs, MoE is a bandwidth play. It drastically reduces B_total in the Roofline equation, bypassing the sub-1 tok/s bottleneck.
📉 Flash Attention & KV Cache
Thesis Finding: Flash Attention causes a 3× performance regression at 15K context depths on CPU. Always use -fa 0.
📊 Empirical Baselines (Pi Family)
• Pi 5 (8GB): ~2.4 tok/s on 7B.
• Pi 4B (4GB): ~0.1 tok/s on 7B (Swap wall).
• Pi 3B (1GB): OOM on 7B. ~10 tok/s on 135M.
